How it works

  1. Switch your wallet to Devnet. Phantom: Settings → Developer Settings → Testnet Mode → Solana Devnet. Solflare: Settings → Network → Devnet.
  2. Grab free devnet SOL from the Solana faucet. It has no value; it's play money.
  3. Pick YES or NO. Your devnet SOL goes into the market's on-chain escrow (a program-owned PDA). Odds are parimutuel: YES% = YES pool ÷ total pool.
  4. After the end time, the market admin resolves it YES, NO, or VOID. Winners split the whole pool pro-rata to their stake. VOID (or a winning side with no bets) refunds everyone.
  5. Claim from My bets. Anyone can trigger payouts, and the money always goes to the bettor's own wallet.
